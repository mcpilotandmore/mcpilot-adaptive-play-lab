The exact expanded phrase did not surface in that screen, but audiences will likely shorten it to MCPilot. This is a material discoverability and originality risk, not formal trademark clearance.

The private rename was deployed as Sites version 20 so the full presentation can be evaluated. Do not mark the public-name freeze complete until the entrant explicitly accepts that risk or selects a more distinctive name.
