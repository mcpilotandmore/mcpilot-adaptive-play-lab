## Claims blocked from public-final wording

- “Judges can access the app” — blocked until signed-out checks pass.
- “The source is public” — blocked until the entrant creates and verifies the public repository.
- “This helped a player” — blocked until the same person completes both trials, explicitly selects Better, the objective result independently qualifies, and the recorded comparison supports the exact wording.
- “Works in Chrome” — blocked until the WebMCP testing flag is enabled and the lifecycle passes there.
- “Accessible” as an unqualified product claim — blocked until real-user acceptance; describe implemented modes and observed automated checks instead.
- “The game cues are audible” — blocked until a person confirms hearing those cues through the active output path.
- “Realtime voice works in production” as an unqualified cross-session or cross-device claim — blocked. The narrower one-run owner-audible claim above is supported; manual Stop, interruption, autoplay recovery, reconnection, unchanged-WebMCP gates, signed-out access, other browsers, and iOS remain untested by a person.
- “Reliable across prompts” — blocked; name the exact prompt classes tested.
